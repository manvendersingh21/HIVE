Why a graph? Machines, operating systems, tools and capabilities are stored as entities and relations rather than a flat list, so placement becomes a query — “which machines have agentic-cli and the most free memory?” — instead of a hardcoded branch. Today there is one Linux worker and the answer is obvious; the shape is what makes the second and third machine cheap to add.
