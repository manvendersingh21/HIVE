Why suspended and not killed? A SIGSTOP is reversible and loses no work: the process keeps its file descriptors, its half-written buffers and its place in the task. That is what makes a human answer worth waiting for — resume is a real option, not a euphemism for "start over". The cost is that a suspended session holds its resources until someone decides, which is why this page exists.
